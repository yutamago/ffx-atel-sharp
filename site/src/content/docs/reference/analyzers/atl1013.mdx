`Self`, `Actors[…]`, `Turn`, `Workers[n]` or another holder of members is kept in a local, passed or returned. Error: the build fails until it is fixed.

## What it means

`Actors[Target.AllPly]` doesn't stand for an object. It only says whom the member after it acts on: the
compiler reads `Actors[Target.AllPly].Defense` as one call of a game function, with the target as its first
argument. Without a member after it, there is nothing to call.
