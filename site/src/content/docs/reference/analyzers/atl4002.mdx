An `[Inject]` points at a call or member the vanilla method doesn't have, or doesn't have that many times. Error: the build fails until it is fixed.

## What it means

`[Inject]` runs your code at the statement holding the n-th place of a call or member in a vanilla method. Here
`OnTurn` calls `Btl.UseCommand` twice, so there is no third place to inject after. The rule also reports an
`[Inject]` that names neither `Before` nor `After`, or both.

## When to ignore it

Never. A game update can move code around: when the vanilla source you build against changes, check your
injects again. The build reports the same error.
